Protect Your Local Search Rankings

Over time, businesses move, change phone numbers, or rebrand. If these changes are not reflected uniformly across the internet, it creates "NAP inconsistency" (Name, Address, Phone). Search engines view this inconsistency as a lack of trustworthiness, which can tank your Local SEO rankings.

Our Citation Audit & Cleanup service is a critical first step before any new citation building. We use advanced tools to scan hundreds of directories and data aggregators, identifying every instance of incorrect or duplicate business information. We then systematically claim, update, or remove these listings to ensure a 100% consistent digital footprint, paving the way for successful Local Citation Services.

Cleanup Process:

  • Comprehensive scan of top data aggregators and directories.
  • Identification and removal of duplicate or suppressed listings.
  • Standardization of NAP data to match your primary GMB profile.
